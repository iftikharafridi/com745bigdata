How each topic works

Every topic page follows the same six parts, so you always know where to look.

PartWhat you do there
A UnderstandThe ideas and why they matter, with diagrams and small explorers next to each idea.
B Watch the demonstrationThe lecturer's step-by-step demos, with the real output of every command.
C ExploreChange inputs, run things and watch what happens.
D PractiseShort class exercises with answers to check.
E Complete independentlyThe official practicals with their original task numbers, hints and troubleshooting.
F Check understandingA quiz with feedback, misconceptions, key terms and a self-check.

Badges tell you what you are looking at: Verified output captured from the real software, Real SQLite engine a real database in the page, Checked with a MongoDB emulator two independent emulators agree, Simulation a teaching model. More about the badges.

Assessment

Coursework 1 · 25%

Practical skills test

60 minutes, open book, on campus in the Week 7 lab: Monday 9 November 2026. Covers general database concepts, relational, NoSQL, document, time-series and graph databases. Assesses LO1.

Revise with Part F of each database topic and keep your practical logs: you may use them in the test.

Coursework 2 · 75%

Data lake project and video

Released Week 4; due Friday 11 December 2026, 12:00. Find public datasets on education level and related deaths, load them into a data lake technology from the module, analyse and visualise them, and present a 15-slide deck with an embedded 5-minute video demonstration. Assesses LO2, LO3, LO4.

The CW2 method page (planned for Week 11) covers each stage with a different worked example.
