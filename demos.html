Each demonstration has
  • Objective, prerequisites, dataset and timing
  • Step-by-step commands with a question to ask the class
  • The result of every command, captured on the real software
  • An explanation to reveal after students predict
  • Common errors and fixes, variations to try live
  • A student practice task (worked solutions are in the separate lecturer pack)
